## Pattern check from the supplied Ahrefs list

The extra Ahrefs list supplied on August 26, 2026 is a 25-keyword fishing
sample filtered to KD 20 or lower. It is **not** hosting or AI-agent demand, so
its volumes are not used for this cluster.

It does confirm five useful page shapes in a real low-difficulty set:

- a plain topic page, such as `palomar knot`;
- a type guide, such as `types of fishing reels`;
- a task tutorial, such as `how to use a baitcaster`;
- a direct comparison, such as `baitcaster vs spinning reel`;
- a `best` page with clear buying intent, such as `best braided fishing line`.

That pattern supports the current mix of a neutral hub, setup tutorials, and a
channel comparison. A `best` hosting or gateway page still stays on hold until
we can compare products with one fair method and show the proof beside every
recommendation.

## Strategic decision

Do not use `messaging gateway` as the SEO hub. Its US search volume is about 40
and its results mix AI-agent docs, software patterns, email security, and
telecom. Use `WhatsApp AI agent` as the hub instead.

The strongest new page is `OpenClaw WhatsApp`:

- US volume: 500;
- KD: 12;
- related setup and integration terms: about 200 each;
- current intent: official docs, setup guides, troubleshooting, and integration
  pages.

VoidFix demand is small or not yet indexed. Build one combined product page and
separate task tutorials. Do not split review, pricing, WhatsApp, and SMS product
research into thin pages.
