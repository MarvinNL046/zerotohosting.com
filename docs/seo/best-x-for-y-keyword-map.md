## Editorial rule

“Best” means the best fit for a documented need and work split. It does not mean a provider won an unperformed speed, support, uptime, or security test.

Each page must include:

- a short answer;
- clear fit and non-fit cases;
- the work owned by the customer and the host;
- a decision table;
- pre-purchase checks;
- official sources and a checked date;
- test limits;
- internal links to the cluster hub and the next useful guide.
