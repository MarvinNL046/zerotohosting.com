# VoidFix Messaging Source Ledger

Checked: **2026-08-26**  
Scope: future English ZeroToHosting pages about VoidFix WhatsApp, VoidFix SMS, and the VoidFix–HighLevel connection.  
Evidence rule: only current primary sources from VoidFix, WhatsApp/Meta, and HighLevel are used here. Ahrefs and search results may guide page intent, but they are not product evidence.

This is an editorial evidence record, not legal advice. Messaging rules depend on the sender, message, recipient, carrier, and country. Recheck the relevant rules before publication and before a material update.

## Status key

- **verified** — the exact operational or policy fact is directly supported by an official, controlling source.
- **provider-stated** — VoidFix states the feature, price, performance, or benefit, but the source alone does not independently prove the outcome.
- **unsupported** — the proposed wording goes beyond, conflicts with, or has no support in the primary evidence reviewed.
- **unknown** — the public primary sources are incomplete, ambiguous, or internally inconsistent; hands-on testing or written clarification is needed.

## Publication gates

1. Attribute every provider-only capability: use “VoidFix says,” “VoidFix lists,” or “VoidFix’s documentation shows.”
2. Put a visible affiliate disclosure before the first paid link. Keep evidence/source links clean; do not add a referral code to a source citation.
3. Recheck all prices, trial terms, plan limits, and affiliate terms within 24 hours of publishing or materially refreshing a page.
4. Recheck WhatsApp’s legal pages after **2026-09-23**. Meta currently announces updated business terms for that date.
5. Do not publish account screenshots containing a QR code, API key, token, phone number, email address, contact/message history, account ID, location ID, sub-account name, or permanent connection URL.
6. Do not reproduce VoidFix’s documentation text or dashboard artwork. VoidFix’s terms restrict commercial republication of its content without written permission. Use original instructions and self-made diagrams; obtain written permission before publishing product/dashboard screenshots.
7. Do not describe this route as “official,” “Meta-approved,” “ban-proof,” “compliance-proof,” or “risk-free.”
